Breakfast sets the tone for your entire day. The right morning meal stabilizes blood sugar, reduces cravings, and gives you energy without the mid-morning crash.

Here are 15 quick, delicious breakfast ideas that support weight loss without leaving you hungry.

## What Makes a Weight-Loss Breakfast?

The ideal weight-loss breakfast has three things:
1. **High protein** (20g+) — keeps you full for 3-4 hours
2. **Fiber** — slows digestion, prevents sugar spikes
3. **Minimal added sugar** — prevents the crash-and-crave cycle

## 15 Breakfast Ideas for Weight Loss

### Quick Options (Under 10 Minutes)

**1. Greek Yogurt Parfait**
Plain Greek yogurt + mixed berries + 1 tbsp chia seeds + a drizzle of honey. ~25g protein, 350 calories.

**2. Egg White Scramble**
3 egg whites + 1 whole egg + spinach + cherry tomatoes. ~22g protein, 200 calories.

**3. Overnight Oats**
Rolled oats + milk + protein powder + banana slices. Prep the night before. ~28g protein, 380 calories.

**4. Cottage Cheese Toast**
Whole grain toast + cottage cheese + sliced cucumber + everything bagel seasoning. ~20g protein, 280 calories.

**5. Protein Smoothie**
Protein powder + frozen spinach + banana + almond milk + nut butter. ~30g protein, 350 calories.

### Slightly More Effort (15-20 Minutes)

**6. Veggie Omelette**
3 eggs + bell peppers + onion + mushrooms + feta. ~25g protein, 320 calories.

**7. Smoked Salmon Toast**
Rye bread + cream cheese + smoked salmon + capers + red onion. ~22g protein, 340 calories.

**8. Avocado Egg Bowls**
Baked eggs inside avocado halves with salsa and lime. ~18g protein, 380 calories.

**9. Turkey Breakfast Wrap**
Whole wheat tortilla + turkey slices + eggs + salsa. ~28g protein, 420 calories.

**10. Quinoa Breakfast Bowl**
Cooked quinoa + almond milk + cinnamon + nuts + fresh fruit. ~15g protein, 380 calories.

### Meal Prep Options (Make in Batch)

**11. Egg Muffins**
Bake eggs with vegetables in a muffin tin. Refrigerate for the week. Per 2 muffins: ~18g protein, 220 calories.

**12. Chia Seed Pudding**
Chia seeds + coconut milk + vanilla, refrigerate overnight. ~10g protein, 280 calories per serving.

**13. Protein Pancakes**
Banana + eggs + oats blended and cooked. ~20g protein, 350 calories for 3 pancakes.

**14. Breakfast Burritos**
Make in bulk, freeze individually. Eggs + turkey + vegetables + whole wheat tortilla. ~25g protein, 380 calories.

**15. Baked Oatmeal**
Large batch baked with berries and nuts. Reheat portions throughout the week. ~12g protein, 350 calories.

## The Breakfasts to Avoid

- **Sugary cereals** — crash within 90 minutes
- **Pastries and muffins** — high calorie, low satiety
- **Fruit juice** — sugar without fiber
- **Most commercial granola bars** — usually glorified candy
