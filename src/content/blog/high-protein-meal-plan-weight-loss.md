High-protein diets are consistently ranked among the most effective strategies for fat loss. Protein keeps you fuller longer, requires more energy to digest, and preserves muscle mass during a caloric deficit.

Here is a complete 7-day high protein meal plan with macros, plus how to customize it to your exact needs.

## Why Protein Matters for Weight Loss

When you eat in a caloric deficit, your body can lose both fat AND muscle. Protein intake of **1.6–2.2g per kg of bodyweight** protects lean muscle while maximizing fat burning.

Additionally, protein has the highest thermic effect of any macronutrient — your body burns **20–30% of protein calories** just through digestion.

## Your Daily Protein Targets

| Goal | Protein Target |
|------|---------------|
| Maintain muscle, lose fat | 1.6g/kg bodyweight |
| Active person (3-5x/week training) | 2.0g/kg bodyweight |
| Maximum muscle preservation | 2.2g/kg bodyweight |

*Example: 70kg person = 112–154g protein per day*

## 7-Day High Protein Meal Plan

### Day 1
- **Breakfast:** Greek yogurt (20g protein) + berries + granola
- **Snack:** 2 hard-boiled eggs + cucumber
- **Lunch:** Grilled chicken breast (35g) + quinoa + roasted vegetables
- **Snack:** Protein shake + apple
- **Dinner:** Salmon fillet (30g) + sweet potato + green beans
- **Total:** ~140g protein, ~1,800 calories

### Day 2
- **Breakfast:** 3-egg omelette with spinach and feta
- **Snack:** Cottage cheese + pineapple
- **Lunch:** Turkey and avocado wrap + side salad
- **Snack:** Handful of almonds + string cheese
- **Dinner:** Lean beef stir-fry with broccoli and brown rice
- **Total:** ~135g protein, ~1,750 calories

### Days 3–7
The pattern continues with variation: rotating protein sources (chicken, fish, turkey, eggs, legumes for vegetarians), different vegetables, and complex carbs timed around activity.
